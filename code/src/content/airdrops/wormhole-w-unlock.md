Wormhole's W 2.0 upgrade in Oct 2025 replaced annual cliff unlocks with bi-weekly releases (smoother supply curve, less concentrated sell pressure). The Core Contributors allocation is the exception — those tokens technically unlock bi-weekly to the Wormhole Foundation but remain time-locked under the original annual contractual schedule, with May 15 as the next contractual-release date.

For W holders, this means May 15 isn't an automatic supply event — it's a date past which Foundation-held contributor tokens become legally usable. Worth noting in the calendar but historically less price-sensitive than open cliff unlocks. Stake-for-governance + 4% yield remains the primary ongoing incentive surface.
