Starknet runs one of the longer-lived L2 airdrop programs — Provisions has cycled through multiple eligibility expansions (early dapp users → Stark Stark participants → various GitHub-contributor / ENS-holder cohorts). Worth re-checking eligibility on each round in case your wallet got re-classified.

The Jun 15 cliff is part of the standard monthly cliff schedule that runs through 2027 — predictable in cadence but each cliff has produced visible short-term selling pressure historically. If you're holding farmed STRK, plan around the cliffs rather than reacting to them.
