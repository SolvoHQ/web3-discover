Optimism's RetroPGF rounds remain one of the most legitimate non-farming OP distribution paths — open-source maintainers, educators, and ecosystem builders have received 5–6 figure OP allocations in prior rounds. Round eligibility refreshes per round, so contribution history matters.

The Jun 30 cliff is the standard monthly unlock to Core Contributors — historically ~2.9% of circulating supply has released on the 30th. As with ARB and STRK, plan around it rather than reacting; the unlock is fully priced into forward markets but each event still produces visible spot-price impact.
