## Architectural note

The `status: ended` enum with `default(active)` means existing entries dont need backfill. Future rugged / ended entries set `status: ended` and they fall out of the index but keep their canonical URL for SEO history.

`deadline: 2026-06-23` on MegaETH means it will naturally sort to the top of the deadline-ascending index — first entry in the directory with a real deadline (rest are all ongoing).
