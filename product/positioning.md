# Positioning — web3-discover

## Brand promise

**The airdrop hunter's honest map.** No scams. No noise. No paid placements pretending to be listings. Just fresh, vetted opportunities you can act on.

## Target user, one sentence

Crypto-native retail user who is already farming airdrops and is tired of spammy aggregator sites, paid-promo pretending-to-be-news, and rugged claim links.

## What we are NOT

- Not a beginner's "how does crypto work" site
- Not a yield farming aggregator (DeFiLlama already wins that)
- Not a price/data terminal (CoinGecko / CMC win that)
- Not a Twitter-noise repackager — every entry is hand-vetted before publishing
- Not a wallet, not custody, not signing — we never touch user funds

## What we ARE

The **trusted curator** for one thing: which airdrops are actually live, what action earns them, when the deadline is.

## Voice

- **Plain English**, not crypto-jargon-by-default
- **Skeptical-friendly**: assume the reader is smart, not credulous; assume listings are scams until proven otherwise
- **Concrete over hype**: never say "potential 100x"; say "snapshot Dec 15, $0 cost, ~5 min effort"
- **First-person plural ("we") sparingly**; mostly second-person ("you can earn…")
- **No emojis in core listings**; emojis OK in occasional editorial / banner copy

## Visual direction

Anti-AI-slop. The default "AI default" web3 look is purple gradient + glow + glassmorphism + Inter — we look like the opposite:

- **Reference aesthetic**: editorial / wire-service. Think Bloomberg Terminal × Hacker News × Craigslist honesty.
- **Density**: information-dense table-first layout. Cards only where genuinely needed.
- **Type**: serif headline (e.g. Source Serif Pro, Newsreader) for editorial trust; mono for any code/address/handle; sans for body.
- **Color**: near-monochrome base + ONE accent color (probably a warm orange or olive — NOT purple, NOT cyan).
- **No glow, no gradient, no glass.**
- **Mobile first**: most users land via Twitter link.

## Differentiation lines vs competitors

- vs **airdrops.io**: they list everything to maximize ad inventory; we filter brutally to maximize trust.
- vs **Cryptorank airdrops tab**: they bundle airdrops as a side feature inside a data terminal; we are a single-purpose tool.
- vs **Twitter airdrop accounts**: they're individuals with paid-promo conflicts; we're a structured, queryable index with a public anti-scam stance.
- vs **Layer3 / Galxe**: those are platforms running quests; we aggregate across them plus protocol-native campaigns.

## Brand expansion path (not v1)

"Discover" is intentionally broader than "airdrops". If airdrops works, the same brand and audience extend to:

- **Grants discovery** — for builders, same "fresh + curated" promise
- **Hackathons** — same audience, lower commerce intent
- **New-protocol watchlist** — first 30 days of mainnet protocols, vetted
- **Honest dapp directory** — chain-by-chain, with explicit "not paid placement" labels

We do NOT advertise these paths publicly until airdrops is working.
